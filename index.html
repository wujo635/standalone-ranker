Undeleting lets an item sync again — it does not restore its past rating or history. Only relevant for categories with identity fields, where re-adding an item with the same title and identity value(s) as something previously deleted would otherwise be permanently blocked from syncing.
Preload from CSV

CSV format guide

Use # prefixed rows to define the schema, followed by a header row and data rows.

#category,NBA Players
#primary,Name
#field,Team,optional
#field,Position,required
#field,Draft Year,optional
Name,Team,Position,Draft Year
LeBron James,Lakers,SF,2003
Stephen Curry,Warriors,PG,2009

Rules: #category and #primary are required. Each #field row defines an extra field and its required/optional status. The header row must match the primary label and field names exactly.
